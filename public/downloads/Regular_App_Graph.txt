# Regular App Account-wise Tab & Function Graph (Excluding Preview Tab)

1. USER ACCOUNT (Traveller Role)
   ├── Bottom Navigation (Global)
   │    ├── Caught Deals (Bidding)
   │    │    └── Bidding for custom travel packages, Accept/Reject quotes
   │    ├── Booking
   │    │    ├── Flights (Search, Seats, Baggage, Meals, Fares, Checkout)
   │    │    ├── Hotels (Search, Details, Checkout)
   │    │    ├── Buses (Search, Seatmap, Checkout)
   │    │    ├── Trains (Search, Checkout)
   │    │    └── Cabs (Search, Checkout)
   │    ├── My Trips (Workspace Navigation)
   │    │    ├── Planning (AI itineraries, Day plans)
   │    │    ├── Expenses (Kharch, Fuel Calculator, Smart Expense Scanner, Budget Dashboard)
   │    │    └── Social (Community Hub, Polls, Memories, Groups)
   │    └── Settings
   │         ├── Profile & Account Settings
   │         ├── Travelport Console (Agent Orchestrator)
   │         ├── Language Onboarding
   │         └── Privacy & Credits
   └── Modals / Floating Features
        ├── AI Chatbot (Antigravity Assistant)
        ├── Music Player (Spotify/Mood integration)
        ├── SOS Button & Emergency Flow
        ├── Security Threat Modal (RASP)
        └── UPI / Payment QR Modal

2. AGENT ACCOUNT (B2B Partner Role)
   └── Agent Portal Dashboard
        ├── Overview & Analytics (Revenue, Bookings summary)
        ├── Offers / Leads (User trip requests, Quote submission)
        ├── Inventory & Packages (Manage custom packages, Pricing, Status)
        ├── Bookings & Leads (Confirmed bookings tracking)
        ├── Earnings & Statement (Payouts, Wallets)
        ├── Markup Engine (Set commission percentages)
        ├── Agency Support (Raise tickets)
        └── Profile & KYC (Settings)

3. ADMIN ACCOUNT (Super Admin Role)
   └── Admin Dashboard
        ├── Platform Analytics (Revenue trends, Active users)
        ├── PentAGI & Codex Security (Threat logs, RASP alerts)
        ├── Vendor Approvals (Pending agent verifications)
        ├── Commission & Payouts (Settle agent wallets)
        ├── Active Ads & Offers (Promo codes, Banners)
        ├── API & System Health (Monitoring third-party APIs)
        ├── Support & Vault (Customer tickets, Vault access)
        └── User Directory (Manage all users, block/unblock)
