Section 5

Flights and Hotels (Travelport-sourced inventory)

When you search for flights or hotels, the Platform queries live availability and pricing from Travelport and, where configured, other connected Suppliers. Selecting an itinerary or room does not constitute a confirmed Booking; a Booking is confirmed only after (a) all required traveller and payment details are submitted, (b) payment is successfully captured, and (c) the Platform receives a confirmed reservation reference (PNR) or hotel confirmation number from the Supplier or GDS. In limited circumstances — including where automated ticketing with the relevant Supplier could not be completed at the time of payment — your Booking may be placed in a "Payment Confirmed, Ticketing Pending" state. In such cases, your payment has been successfully received and secured, and our operations team will complete manual ticketing and send you confirmation within a reasonable time (ordinarily within 24 hours). If ticketing cannot be completed at the originally quoted fare due to a Supplier-side fare or availability change, you will be offered the choice of an alternative fare/itinerary or a full refund.

Section 6

Buses, Trains, and Car Rentals

Bus, train, and car-rental inventory is sourced through our aggregator integration. Seat selection, blocking, and ticketing for buses proceeds through a two-step block-then-book process; a seat block does not guarantee a seat until booking is confirmed and payment is captured within the applicable hold window. Train bookings are subject to the rules, fare structure, and cancellation framework of the Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC) and Indian Railways, which take precedence over any conflicting provision of these Terms.

Section 8

RTAIP — AI-Assisted Trip Planning

RTAIP is an artificial-intelligence feature that suggests itineraries, binds them to live Supplier inventory where available, and can orchestrate multi-step bookings (flights and, where supported, hotels) on your behalf once you authorise payment. You acknowledge and agree to the following: Itinerary suggestions generated by RTAIP are recommendations only and do not constitute a confirmed Booking until the underlying inventory has been bound to a live Supplier offer and payment has been processed. Where RTAIP has bound your itinerary to a verified, live Supplier offer, the checkout will proceed to real ticketing; where such an offer could not be verified, RTAIP will not fabricate a confirmation, and your Booking will be marked "Payment Confirmed, Ticketing Pending" as described in Section 5.1, with manual completion by our team. You are responsible for reviewing all AI-suggested itinerary details (dates, passenger counts, room configurations, class of travel) before authorising payment. RouTripo is not liable for losses arising from a User's failure to review and correct AI-suggested details prior to payment. RTAIP may use third-party large-language-model providers to generate itinerary text and recommendations; no payment card or government-ID information is shared with such providers.
