Section 1

General Principles

RouTripo acts as an intermediary facilitating your Booking with independent Suppliers (airlines, hotels, bus operators, train services, car-rental companies, and holiday-package providers). Cancellation eligibility, cancellation charges, and refund amounts are, in the first instance, governed by the specific fare rules, rate rules, or tariff conditions of the relevant Supplier applicable to your Booking, which are displayed to you prior to payment and which you accept at the time of Booking. This Policy explains the general framework within which such Supplier-specific rules operate, and the additional terms specific to RouTripo's own service/convenience fee and Wallet credit. Where a Booking is cancelled, any RouTripo service or convenience fee charged at the time of Booking is non-refundable, except where the cancellation arises from a verified error, system fault, or double-charge on RouTripo's part, or from RouTripo's or a Supplier's failure to fulfil a confirmed Booking.

Section 2

Flights

Cancellation charges for flight Bookings are levied by the airline in accordance with the fare rules applicable to the fare class purchased, and vary by airline, route, and how far in advance of departure the cancellation is made. The applicable fare rules and cancellation-charge slabs are displayed at the time of Booking and are also retrievable through the "Fare Rules" link on your Booking confirmation. Refundable-fare Bookings are eligible for a refund of the base fare less the airline's cancellation charge and RouTripo's service fee, credited to your original payment method within 7—15 business days of cancellation, subject to the airline's own refund-processing timeline (which, for certain international carriers, may take longer). Non-refundable/promotional fares are not eligible for a fare refund on voluntary cancellation, though statutory airport taxes and certain airline-specific fees may be refundable upon request, in accordance with the airline's policy. No-show (failure to check in or board without prior cancellation) forfeits the entire fare in most fare classes, in accordance with the airline's no-show policy. If your flight is cancelled or significantly rescheduled by the airline itself, you are entitled to a full refund of the amount paid to the airline (excluding RouTripo's non-refundable service fee, save where RouTripo determines a service-fee waiver is warranted), or, at your election and where offered by the airline, rebooking on an alternate available flight at no additional fare difference.

Section 3

Hotels and Stays

Each hotel rate displayed on the Platform is tagged as either "Free Cancellation" (up to a stated date/time) or "Non-Refundable". You should confirm the applicable rate type before completing payment. For a "Free Cancellation" rate, cancelling before the stated deadline entitles you to a full refund of the room amount paid, less RouTripo's non-refundable service fee. Cancelling after the deadline, or a no-show at check-in, will ordinarily forfeit one night's charge or the full stay amount, depending on the specific rate rule shown at Booking. "Non-Refundable" rates are not eligible for a cash refund on cancellation or no-show, regardless of the reason for cancellation, save where required by applicable consumer-protection law or where the hotel itself is unable to honour the Booking (in which case a full refund or comparable alternative accommodation will be arranged). Amendments to check-in/check-out dates or room type are treated as a new Booking request subject to then-current rate and availability, and may not be possible on a non-amendable rate.

Buses

Refunds, where applicable, are processed to your original payment method within 5—7 business days after the operator confirms the cancellation.

Section 5

Trains

Train Bookings made through the Platform are subject to the cancellation rules, Tatkal charges, and the Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) framework prescribed by Indian Railways and IRCTC, which take precedence over any conflicting provision of this Policy. Cancellation charges are deducted per berth/class as per the railway's published slab (based on time before scheduled departure), and refunds for a cancelled confirmed ticket, a waitlisted ticket that could not be confirmed, or a train cancelled/delayed by Indian Railways are processed in accordance with IRCTC's refund rules and timelines, which may take up to 7 working days or longer as determined by IRCTC. RouTripo will assist in filing a TDR on your behalf where required, but the refund decision and timeline rest with Indian Railways/IRCTC.

Section 6

Car Rentals

Cancellation charges for self-drive or chauffeur-driven car rentals are set by the rental provider and are displayed at the time of Booking, typically on a sliding scale based on the time remaining before the scheduled pickup. A security deposit, where collected by the rental provider at pickup, is refunded by the rental provider directly in accordance with its own terms and is outside RouTripo's control. No-show at the scheduled pickup time, or failure to produce a valid driving licence/identification at pickup, will ordinarily forfeit the full rental amount.

Section 7

Holiday Packages

Holiday packages typically require an advance payment at the time of Booking, with the balance due by a specified date before departure. Cancellation of a holiday package is subject to a tiered forfeiture schedule based on how far in advance of the travel date the cancellation is made (for example, a higher percentage of the total package cost is forfeited for cancellations made closer to the departure date), as disclosed to you at the time of Booking for that specific package. Where the package includes third-party components (flights, visa-processing fees, or non-refundable activity bookings), those components' own non-refundable status will apply in addition to RouTripo's package cancellation schedule.

Section 8

RTAIP AI-Booked Trips

Where a Booking made through the RTAIP feature is placed in a "Payment Confirmed, Ticketing Pending" state because automated ticketing with the Supplier could not be completed at checkout, and our team is subsequently unable to complete ticketing at the originally quoted fare or itinerary within a reasonable time, you are entitled to a full refund of the amount paid for that Booking, including any RouTripo service fee, credited to your original payment method within 7 business days of RouTripo confirming that ticketing could not be completed.

Section 9

Bargaining/Bidding Bookings

Cancellation of a Booking made through the Bargaining feature, after a bid has been accepted and escrow payment made, is governed by the cancellation terms specific to that feature, set out in our Bargaining & Bidding Policy, given the escrow-hold and vendor-lock mechanics unique to that flow.

Section 10

How to Request a Cancellation

Cancellations can be requested through the "My Trips" section of the Platform, by selecting the relevant Booking and choosing "Cancel Booking". Where an in-app cancellation option is not available for a particular Booking type, you may contact our support team at support@RouTripo.com or through the in-app help/support chat, quoting your Booking reference number. A cancellation is effective from the date and time RouTripo (or, where applicable, the Supplier) receives and processes your cancellation request, not from the date and time you initiate contact.

Section 11

Refund Processing

Eligible refunds are credited to the original payment method used for the Booking. RouTripo does not issue refunds in cash or to a different payment method/bank account than the one used for payment, except where a refund is credited to your RouTripo Wallet at your explicit request. Refund timelines stated in this Policy are indicative and commence from the date the cancellation is confirmed by the relevant Supplier (where applicable); actual credit to your account is also subject to your bank's or card network's own processing time, which is outside RouTripo's control. Where a refund is delayed beyond the timelines stated in this Policy for reasons attributable to RouTripo, you may escalate the matter to our Grievance Officer as detailed in our Terms & Conditions.

Section 12

Force Majeure Cancellations

Where a Booking cannot be fulfilled due to circumstances beyond RouTripo's or the Supplier's reasonable control (including natural disasters, pandemics, government-imposed travel restrictions, or civil unrest), refund or credit eligibility will follow the specific force-majeure policy announced by the relevant Supplier or by RouTripo for that event, which will be communicated to affected Users as soon as reasonably possible.
