Section 1

Introduction

RouTripo ("RouTripo", "we", "us", "our") recognises the importance of your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us as a User, Agent, or Partner of the RouTripo platform ("Platform"). This Privacy Policy describes what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights available to you. This Privacy Policy applies to any person who accesses, registers on, browses, or transacts through the Platform, including our website, mobile application, and any connected AI trip-planning (RTAIP) or Bargaining features. This Privacy Policy is incorporated into, and forms part of, our Terms & Conditions. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given to them in the Terms & Conditions. For a detailed account of your rights and our obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, please also refer to our separate DPDP Compliance Policy, which supplements and, in the event of any conflict specifically relating to statutory rights under that Act, prevails over this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy does not apply to the practices of third parties that RouTripo does not own or control, including independent Suppliers, Agents, and Partners, or to individuals RouTripo does not employ or manage. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any Supplier or Agent you transact with.

Section 2

Information you provide directly

Account information: name, email address, phone number, password (stored in hashed form), and profile photograph. Booking information: traveller names, date of birth, gender, government-issued identification numbers where required by a Supplier (e.g., passport number for international flights, ID for train travel), frequent-flyer numbers, and special requirements (e.g., meal preference, wheelchair assistance). Payment-related information: billing name and address, and the last four digits of a payment card for your reference. RouTripo does not store your full card number, CVV, or expiry date; full payment-card data is captured directly by our payment gateway partner (Razorpay), which is independently PCI-DSS certified. Agent/Partner KYC information: for Agents and Partners, business registration documents, GSTIN, PAN, bank account details for settlement, and identity-verification documents submitted during onboarding. Communications: messages you send through the in-app Bargaining chat, customer-support conversations, and any content you submit as a review, rating, or trip-social post. RTAIP inputs: trip preferences, budget ranges, and travel-style prompts you provide to the AI trip-planning feature.

Section 3

Information collected automatically

Device information: device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile-app version. Usage information: pages/screens viewed, search queries, click-stream data, and crash/error logs (via our error-monitoring tooling). Location information: with your permission, precise or approximate location data used to show nearby hotels, personalise search defaults, or provide SOS/safety features during a trip. Cookies and similar technologies: as described in Section 8 below.

Section 4

Information from third parties

If you sign in using Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photograph from that provider, subject to your consent at the time of sign-in. Suppliers and GDS partners (including Travelport) may share booking-status and fare-confirmation data back to us to complete and display your Booking.

Section 5

How We Use Your Information (Purpose and Legal Basis)

We collect and process your personal information for the following purposes, on the legal bases of (a) your consent, (b) performance of the contract you enter into with us when you make a Booking, and (c) our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Platform, each as applicable and as further detailed in our DPDP Compliance Policy: To create and manage your account and authenticate your identity; To process, confirm, and manage your Bookings, including sharing necessary traveller details with the relevant Supplier or Agent to fulfil the Booking; To process payments and refunds through our payment gateway partner; To operate the Bargaining feature, including matching your Trip Request with responding Agents while masking your direct contact details from Agents until a deal is accepted and locked; To power RTAIP's itinerary suggestions and, where you authorise it, to complete automated booking on your behalf; To send you Booking confirmations, itinerary updates, and service-related communications (transactional messages are not optional and are sent regardless of marketing-communication preferences); To send you promotional offers and marketing communications, where you have opted in to receive them, and to allow you to opt out at any time; To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse of the Bargaining anti-leakage system, or other violations of our Terms; To comply with applicable law, including tax/GST invoicing obligations, and to respond to lawful requests from public authorities; To improve the Platform through aggregated and, wherever feasible, de-identified analytics.

Section 6

With Whom We Share Your Information

We share your personal information only as necessary for the purposes described above, and with the following categories of recipients: Suppliers and GDS partners (e.g., Travelport, airlines, hotels, train and bus operators, car-rental companies) — to the extent necessary to create and fulfil your Booking; Payment gateway partner (Razorpay) — to process your payment and any refund; Agents/Partners responding to your Trip Request via the Bargaining feature — limited to the trip requirements you have shared, with your direct contact details (phone number, email) masked and withheld until a bid is accepted and the deal is locked, in accordance with our Bargaining & Bidding Policy; Cloud infrastructure and technology sub-processors (including our hosting, database, error-monitoring, and AI/LLM providers) who process data solely on our instructions and under contractual confidentiality obligations; Government authorities, law-enforcement agencies, or courts, where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to that entity agreeing to honour the commitments made in this Privacy Policy. RouTripo does not sell your personal information to third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

Section 7

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including transport-layer encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit, envelope encryption for particularly sensitive stored fields using distinct per-user data-encryption keys, role-based access controls limiting internal access on a need-to-know basis, and rate-limiting and anomaly-detection safeguards on our booking and payment endpoints. While we take these obligations seriously, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 8

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including for the duration of your account's active status, and thereafter for such additional period as required to comply with our legal obligations (including tax, accounting, and GST-invoicing record-keeping requirements, which may require retention of booking and payment records for a period prescribed under applicable law), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Chat messages exchanged during Bargaining negotiations are retained for a limited period after deal completion or expiry for dispute-resolution and safety purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

Section 9

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights in relation to your personal information, which you may exercise by writing to us at the contact details in Section 11, or (where available) through the relevant in-app settings screen: Right to access a copy of the personal information we hold about you; Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information; Right to request erasure of your personal information, subject to our right to retain information necessary for legal, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes as described in Section 6; Right to withdraw consent for a specific processing purpose at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before such withdrawal; Right to nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity; Right to lodge a grievance with our Grievance Officer (Section 11) and, if unresolved, to escalate the matter to the Data Protection Board of India. A withdrawal of consent that is essential for us to provide a specific service (for example, sharing traveller details with an airline to complete ticketing) may mean we are unable to complete or service that particular Booking. Where a Booking has already been confirmed, withdrawal of consent will not itself reverse or cancel that Booking.

Section 10

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies on our website and mobile application to keep you signed in, remember your search preferences, measure Platform performance, and, where you have consented, to personalise offers. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features of the Platform.

Section 11

Permissions on the Mobile Application

Our mobile application may request the following device permissions, each of which is optional except where noted, and each of which you may revoke at any time through your device settings (revoking a permission may disable the corresponding feature): Location — to show nearby hotels/services and to support in-trip SOS/safety features; Camera and Photo Library — to upload a profile photo, scan travel documents, or attach images to a review or expense entry; Contacts — only if you choose to invite a travel companion or share an itinerary; Notifications — to send Booking confirmations, price-drop alerts, and Bargaining chat updates; Microphone — only if you use voice-based search or the AI trip-planning voice input.

Section 12

Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from, individuals under the age of 18 for the purpose of creating an independent account. Where a minor is included as a traveller on a Booking made by an adult User, such information is processed solely for the purpose of completing that Booking with the relevant Supplier, with the consent of the adult User as the minor's parent/lawful guardian.
