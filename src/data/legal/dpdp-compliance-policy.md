## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Policy sets out the framework through which RouTripo, as a "Data Fiduciary" under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), determines the purpose and means of processing Digital Personal Data of its Users, Agents, and Partners (each a "Data Principal"). This Policy supplements our general Privacy Policy and, in the event of any conflict specifically concerning a Data Principal's statutory rights or RouTripo's statutory obligations under the DPDP Act, this Policy shall prevail.
This Policy applies to the processing of digital personal data collected by RouTripo in connection with the Platform, whether collected in digital form or collected in non-digital form and subsequently digitised, in accordance with Section 3 of the DPDP Act.

## 3. Notice and Consent Framework

In accordance with Sections 5 and 6 of the DPDP Act, RouTripo provides itemised notice, in clear and plain language, of the personal data being collected and the specific purpose for which it is being processed, at or before the time such consent is sought. Consent is obtained through an affirmative, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous action (such as ticking a consent checkbox or tapping "I Agree") for each of the following categories of processing, which are sought separately and not bundled into a single blanket consent:
Account creation and identity verification;
Processing of Booking and traveller information to fulfil a specific Booking with a Supplier;
Payment processing through our payment gateway partner;
Marketing communications (opt-in, separately revocable at any time without affecting other consents);
Location access for nearby-service discovery and in-trip safety features (device-level, separately revocable);
Sharing of trip requirements with Agents via the Bargaining feature.
You may withdraw consent for any of the above purposes at any time, with the ease of withdrawal being comparable to the ease with which consent was originally given, by writing to privacy@RouTripo.com or through the relevant in-app settings screen. As explained in our Privacy Policy, withdrawal of consent for processing that is necessary to fulfil an already-confirmed Booking will not reverse that Booking, but will apply to future processing.

## 4. Purpose Limitation and Data Minimisation

RouTripo processes personal data only for the specific purposes for which consent has been obtained, or as otherwise permitted under Section 7 of the DPDP Act ("legitimate uses", including where a Data Principal has voluntarily provided data for a specified purpose and has not indicated non-consent to its use for that purpose, and compliance with law). We collect only such personal data as is necessary for the stated purpose; for example, government-identification numbers are collected only where a specific Supplier (such as an international airline or the railways) mandates them for that Booking, and are not collected as a default field for every Booking type.

## 5. Storage Limitation and Erasure

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or for such longer period as required to comply with a legal obligation applicable to RouTripo (including tax and GST record-keeping requirements). Where a Data Principal withdraws consent or requests erasure, and RouTripo has no other legal ground to continue processing, RouTripo will erase the personal data, and will further cause its Data Processors to likewise erase such data, without unreasonable delay, unless retention is required by law.

## 6. Security Safeguards

In accordance with Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act, RouTripo has implemented reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal-data breaches, including encryption of sensitive data at rest and in transit, per-user data-encryption keys under an envelope-encryption scheme, role-based access controls, rate-limiting on booking and payment endpoints, and logging/monitoring to detect anomalous access. These measures are reviewed periodically as part of our ongoing security posture.

## 7. Data Principal Rights

Under Sections 11 through 14 of the DPDP Act, you have the following rights, which you may exercise by writing to our Grievance Officer at the contact details in Section 11 of this Policy:

## 7.1 Right to Access Information (Section 11)

You have the right to obtain a summary of the personal data being processed by RouTripo and the processing activities undertaken with respect to that data, and identities of all Data Processors with whom the personal data has been shared, together with a description of the data so shared.

## 7.2 Right to Correction and Erasure (Section 12)

You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, completion of incomplete personal data, updating of personal data, and erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, unless retention is required for a legal purpose.

## 7.3 Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13)

You have the right to have any grievance in respect of RouTripo's processing of your personal data or its exercise of your rights resolved by RouTripo within such time as prescribed. You must first exhaust this grievance-redressal process with RouTripo before approaching the Data Protection Board of India.

## 7.4 Right to Nominate (Section 14)

You have the right to nominate any other individual to exercise your rights under the DPDP Act in the event of your death or incapacity, by writing to us with the nominee's details.

## 8. Significant Data Fiduciary Considerations

RouTripo monitors the volume and sensitivity of personal data it processes, and the applicable thresholds and criteria for classification as a "Significant Data Fiduciary" under Section 10 of the DPDP Act. In the event RouTripo is notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary, it will, as required, appoint a Data Protection Officer based in India, appoint an independent data auditor, and undertake periodic Data Protection Impact Assessments, and will update this Policy accordingly.

## 9. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Personal data collected by RouTripo is primarily stored and processed within India. Where personal data is transferred to a jurisdiction outside India — for example, to a cloud infrastructure region or an AI/LLM provider located outside India, or to an international airline/hotel Supplier for the purpose of ticketing — such transfer is undertaken in accordance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act and any rules, restrictions, or country-specific conditions notified by the Central Government from time to time.

## 10. Personal Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal-data breach, RouTripo will, in accordance with Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act and rules made thereunder, notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals, providing details of the nature and extent of the breach, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to be taken to mitigate risk, within the timeframe prescribed under applicable rules.
