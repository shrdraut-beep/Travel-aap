## 1. Overview of the Feature

The Bargaining feature ("Bargaining") is a marketplace mechanism within the RouTripo Platform through which a User ("Requester") posts a Trip Request describing their travel requirements (route, dates, budget range, vehicle/room/vertical preference), to which one or more onboarded Agents/Partners ("Vendors") may respond with a competitive bid ("Offer"). The Requester may negotiate with one or more Vendors through an in-app chat, compare Offers, and accept a single Offer, which is then secured through an escrow-based payment before being locked as a confirmed Booking. This Policy governs the rights and obligations of both Requesters and Vendors using this feature, and is incorporated into, and forms part of, our Terms & Conditions.

## 2. How Bargaining Works — Step by Step

Trip Request: The Requester submits a Trip Request specifying trip details, indicative budget, and any specific requirements. The Trip Request is visible to eligible Vendors, without disclosing the Requester's name, phone number, or email address.
Vendor Offers: Eligible Vendors submit an Offer against the Trip Request within the response window shown on the Platform. Each Offer is masked to display the Vendor's business category and indicative profile (e.g., rating, verification badge) without disclosing the Vendor's direct contact details to the Requester at this stage.
Negotiation: The Requester may open an in-app chat with one or more Vendors to negotiate price, inclusions, or terms. All chat messages pass through an automated content-safety and contact-detail-masking system (see Section 4) before being delivered.
Auction/Response Timer: Where displayed, a countdown timer indicates the window within which the Requester must accept an Offer before it, or the overall Trip Request auction, expires. An expired Offer or Trip Request must be re-initiated by the Requester.
Accept & Lock: On accepting an Offer, the Requester proceeds to a comparison screen confirming the accepted Offer against the original Trip Request, followed by payment. On successful payment, the deal is "locked": the Vendor's real, verified business name and confirmation details are revealed to the Requester, a voucher/confirmation is generated, and the Booking is confirmed with that Vendor.
Escrow Hold: Payment made at the Accept & Lock stage is held by RouTripo in escrow and is not released to the Vendor until the criteria in Section 5 are met.

## 3. Requester Obligations

You must provide accurate and complete trip requirements in your Trip Request. RouTripo and Vendors are not responsible for an unsuitable Offer resulting from incomplete or inaccurate information you provided.
Once you accept an Offer and complete payment, you are bound to that Booking on the terms of the accepted Offer, subject to the cancellation terms in Section 7 below.
You must not use the in-app chat to solicit a Vendor's direct contact details, or to arrange payment or service delivery outside the Platform, for the purpose of avoiding RouTripo's applicable service fee. Any such attempt may result in suspension of your access to the Bargaining feature or your account, in accordance with Section 13 of our Terms & Conditions.

## 4. Anti-Leakage and Chat Safety

To protect both Requesters and Vendors, and to preserve the integrity of the masked-identity negotiation process, all messages exchanged through the Bargaining chat are automatically screened by RouTripo's content-safety system before delivery. This system is designed to detect and block phone numbers, email addresses, external messaging-app handles, and similar contact information from being exchanged before a deal is locked. A message that is blocked or redacted for this reason will be flagged to the sender within the chat. This screening is a safety and platform-integrity measure and is not a substitute for your own judgment in what you choose to share once a deal is locked and direct contact details are appropriately exchanged for service coordination.

## 5. Escrow Mechanism

Payment made on accepting an Offer is held by RouTripo, via our payment gateway partner's escrow/nodal-account mechanism, and is not released to the Vendor immediately on payment.
Funds are released to the Vendor once the relevant service (the trip, stay, or transport covered by the Offer) is confirmed as commenced or delivered, or upon expiry of the applicable dispute window following the scheduled service date — whichever occurs first under the settlement rule configured for that Offer category.
If the Requester raises a valid dispute (Section 8) before funds are released, disbursement to the Vendor is paused pending resolution.
Vendors acknowledge that amounts held in escrow are not treated as settled revenue until released in accordance with this Section and the Vendor settlement terms provided at onboarding.

## 6. Vendor Obligations

A Vendor's Offer must be an accurate, honourable quote for the service described; a Vendor may not knowingly submit an Offer it does not intend to honour if accepted.
Once an Offer is accepted and locked by a Requester, the Vendor is contractually bound to deliver the service on the terms of that Offer, subject to the cancellation terms in Section 7.
Vendors must not use the in-app chat to solicit the Requester's direct contact details before a deal is locked for the purpose of completing the transaction outside the Platform.
Vendors are independent contractors and are solely responsible for the lawful and safe delivery of the service offered. RouTripo verifies Vendor onboarding documentation (including business registration and, where applicable, GSTIN) but does not guarantee, and is not liable for, the quality of service independently rendered by a Vendor.

## 7. Cancellation of a Locked Bargaining Booking

Cancellation by the Requester after a deal is locked is subject to a cancellation charge disclosed at the time of Accept & Lock, which may be higher than a standard catalogue Booking given the bespoke nature of a negotiated Offer, and which will be clearly shown before you confirm cancellation.
Cancellation or non-fulfilment by the Vendor entitles the Requester to a full refund of the escrowed amount, and may additionally affect the Vendor's standing/rating on the Platform.
Where a dispute is raised regarding service quality or non-delivery, escrowed funds will not be released to the Vendor until the dispute is resolved under Section 8.

## 8. Dispute Resolution Specific to Bargaining

A Requester who believes a Vendor has not honoured the terms of a locked Offer may raise a dispute through the Platform within 48 hours of the scheduled service date (or such other window as displayed for that Booking). RouTripo will review the Bargaining chat history, the terms of the accepted Offer, and any evidence submitted by either party, and will determine whether to release the escrowed funds to the Vendor, refund the Requester in full or in part, or seek further information from either party. This process operates in addition to, and does not replace, the general Grievance Redressal mechanism described in our Terms & Conditions.

## 9. Fees and Commission

RouTripo charges a service fee to the Requester and/or a commission to the Vendor on a Bargaining transaction, disclosed to each party at the relevant stage (to the Requester at Accept & Lock, and to the Vendor at onboarding/settlement). These fees are separate from, and in addition to, the negotiated Offer price, and are non-refundable except where the underlying Booking itself is cancelled due to a RouTripo-side error or a Vendor's non-fulfilment.

## 10. Prohibited Conduct

In addition to the general prohibited-conduct provisions in our Terms & Conditions, the following are specifically prohibited within the Bargaining feature:
Attempting to bypass the contact-masking system described in Section 4, whether by encoding, obfuscating, or otherwise disguising contact information in chat messages;
Creating multiple Trip Requests or Vendor accounts to manipulate the auction/bidding process;
Submitting a bid or accepting an Offer with no genuine intention to honour it;
Coordinating with another Vendor to fix or inflate bid pricing on a specific Trip Request.
A violation of this Section may result in forfeiture of the applicable Offer, reversal of an in-progress transaction prior to service delivery, and suspension or termination of the violating party's access to the Bargaining feature or their RouTripo account.

## 11. Relationship of the Parties

RouTripo's role in a Bargaining transaction is limited to that of a technology platform and escrow facilitator connecting Requesters and Vendors. RouTripo is not a party to the underlying contract for travel services formed between the Requester and the Vendor upon acceptance of an Offer, and does not itself act as the travel-service provider in that transaction.
