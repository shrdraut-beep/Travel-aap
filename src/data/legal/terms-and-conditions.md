## 5.1 Flights and Hotels (Travelport-sourced inventory)

When you search for flights or hotels, the Platform queries live availability and pricing from Travelport and, where configured, other connected Suppliers. Selecting an itinerary or room does not constitute a confirmed Booking; a Booking is confirmed only after (a) all required traveller and payment details are submitted, (b) payment is successfully captured, and (c) the Platform receives a confirmed reservation reference (PNR) or hotel confirmation number from the Supplier or GDS.
In limited circumstances — including where automated ticketing with the relevant Supplier could not be completed at the time of payment — your Booking may be placed in a "Payment Confirmed, Ticketing Pending" state. In such cases, your payment has been successfully received and secured, and our operations team will complete manual ticketing and send you confirmation within a reasonable time (ordinarily within 24 hours). If ticketing cannot be completed at the originally quoted fare due to a Supplier-side fare or availability change, you will be offered the choice of an alternative fare/itinerary or a full refund.
